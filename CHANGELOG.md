# Changelog

## 26 October 2026

**Item Collection (Codex) is live.**
Register items to complete collections and earn permanent stat bonuses, shared by all characters of your account. Registering an item has a fee, shown before you confirm; Collector's Gift tickets are free.

**Item grade borders.**
Item slots now show a colored border matching the item's grade, animated on higher grades, and the tooltip frame is tinted to match.

**Collection items from monsters.**
Items needed by the regular collections can now be obtained from monsters, so every regular collection can be completed.

**Daily quests: later steps.**
The last steps of the daily cleanup quests now also give the new experience rewards.

**Hunting Points → CC exchange.**
The Hunter's Flem in each HQ now lets you trade your Hunting Points for CC, with several tiers to choose from (weekly limit per account).

**Daily quests: more XP.**
Daily quest rewards now give much more experience, high levels included.

**Blue Powder.**
Blue Powder is now sold by the Tool merchant in each HQ.

**Hunter's Flem recipe boxes.**
Back on sale, with a weekly limit per account.

**Battle Dungeons.**
Hunting Points are no longer earned inside Battle Dungeons.

**Game closing at character selection.**
Since the previous update, the game could close on its own just before the character list appeared. This is fixed.

**Death menu.**
All revive potions now work from the death menu, and its second button behaves as expected.

**Locked items.**
The padlock now stays on the right item, and a locked guard tower shows a clear message.

**Translations.**
More item names and descriptions fixed.

## 25 October 2026 — second update

**Death screen after changing character.**
The death screen now shows up correctly after going back to character selection without closing the game.

**Locked items.**
You can now equip, unequip and use your locked items. They still cannot be traded, sold, dropped or stored.

**Inventory: crafting very fast.**
Fixed stacks splitting into single slots when a stack fills up while crafting quickly.

**Chip War times.**
After the switch to winter time, Chip War started one hour early. The times are back to normal.

**Collection Inventory Update Package.**
Now limited to one per account, as on the official servers.

**Translations.**
Fixed several item names, hints and messages in all languages.

**Game client performance.**
General performance improvements in the game module.

**Premium Benefits icon.**
The Premium Benefits button now shows its proper icon.

## 25 October 2026

**New game module, installed by the launcher.**

**Buff NPC: new buff shop window.**
The buff NPCs now open a proper buff shop window instead of the old menu.

**Item lock.**
You can now lock an item so it cannot be sold, dropped or stored by mistake.

**Death menu, Chip War timer and radar button.**
The death menu and the Chip War timer now look and work like in the 4.55 version, with a timer button next to the radar.

**Premium icon, ammo tooltips and visual effects.**
The premium status has its own icon and tooltip, ammo tooltips show the attack bonus in percent, and more visual effects now display properly.

**Targeting.**
"Character targeting has failed" no longer repeats on monsters that just died or that others are fighting.

**Auction House: re-listing equipment.**
Putting an expired piece of equipment back on sale no longer fails.

**Crafting: ammo.**
Ammo crafts no longer fail.

**Animus experience.**
Finishing a monster with a low-level Hecate or Isis now gives you the experience it should, and Hecate and Isis now level at the same speed as the other animus.

**Loot and drop rates.**
Loot was adjusted on a few monsters, and the Autumn Crystal drop rate was lowered.

**Bio Lab 2F.**
Spector and Spector Junior respawn faster.

**Cash Shop.**
New items are on sale and a few old ones are back.

**New daily quests for levels 56 to 80.**

**Mining gives more Gold Boxes.**

**Auction House: the "register failed" error is fixed.**
Listing an item could fail with an error. It now works.

**Auction House pager.**
Each use of the disposable pager takes two charges. Its price has been halved, so each use now costs what it should.

**Premium (PC Cafe) rewards between levels 30 and 66.**
Some classes were offered only rings, or the same amulet twice, in the second reward choice. Every class now gets the full choice of amulets.

**Healing potions and item descriptions.**
Ten healing potions applied a buff instead of healing you: they heal again. Many potion descriptions were wrong (including warnings saying the item would be deleted): they have been corrected, and a food item no longer shows the tooltip of another item.

Experience rates are currently raised for a test period. During this test, the extra bonus at levels 51 to 53 announced on 17 October is paused.

## 18 October 2026

**Vote rewards are live.**
Voting for the server now gives Cash Coins: 20 per vote, once a day on each rewarded site, credited automatically to your account within a few minutes. Open the Vote page on the website, type your character name, and use the vote buttons it gives you: a vote cast from a plain banner carries no name and cannot be rewarded. One reward per site per day for each game account.

**Isis and Hecate rebalanced.**
At the same level and gear, Hecate was hitting about twice as hard as Isis, the reverse of the intended design: Isis is meant to be the stronger attacker and Hecate the area-damage support. Isis now hits harder than Hecate on monsters and players alike, and Hecate keeps her area damage. This applies to all animus levels, sealed animus included.

## 17 October 2026

**Dalant limit raised to 4 billion, with automatic Ingots of Dalant.**
You can now carry up to 4,000,000,000 Dalant. Everything above 1,500,000,000 is automatically turned into Ingots of Dalant (500,000,000 each) when you log in, every minute while you play and when you log out, as long as your bag has room; ingots stack on the ones you already carry. Below 2,000,000,000 nothing is converted. Cash an ingot at the Ingot Exchange (up to 1,999,999,999 carried), or buy one there. Do not sell ingots to a regular shop: it only pays 450,000,000.

**Ingots in the bank are free to withdraw.**
Taking Ingots of Dalant out of the bank no longer costs anything.

**Trading Dalant.**
A player cannot receive Dalant by trade if it would put them above 2,000,000,000. Trade ingots instead: they have no limit.

**Auction House: up to 1,999,999,999, and no more disconnections.**
The maximum price is raised to 1,999,999,999 Dalant. Buying an item listed at 1 billion or more no longer disconnects the buyer, and the race/guild share of the tax is now correct on very expensive sales. Sellers always get paid, even offline; if a sale would push a seller above the limit, the purchase is refused with a clear message and nobody loses anything. Update your game through the launcher.

**Talic Upgraders on level 70+ equipment.**
Talic Upgraders (N, B, C, D) now work on level 70 and above equipment with slots (Baalzebub, Rare M, Dragonborn, cloaks, jetpacks and more), with the same success rates as level 70 equipment.

**Almighty Beeper.**
The Storage and Item Combination buttons now work.

**Leveling at 51-53.**
Monsters give 1.5 times more XP at levels 51 to 53 (quest rewards unchanged). On Outcast Land, level 58 to 62 monsters respawn twice as fast. Kills made by your animus give you XP again on monsters of your level or slightly below.

**New gem sets in the Cash Shop.**
The Brilliant, Radiant and Heavenly gem sets can now be bought in the Cash Shop.

**Two new shield combinations.**
Two combinations from the official server are now available for the level 75 Baalzebub shields.

**MAU: defense now follows the armor parts.**
A MAU's defense now depends on the level of its head, body and leg parts: the better the parts, the tougher the MAU. Goliath and Catapult both gain defense. Against monsters, MAU deal their original damage again; against players, nothing changes.

**Anti-MAU traps now work.**
Anti-MAU traps had no effect on MAU until now. They now lower the defense of a MAU caught in the blast, and its pilot is told so in a message (no buff icon is shown).

**MAU Blessing drops again.**
Stocker Lava in Volcanic Cauldron and Sand StingBug on Outcast Land drop MAU Blessing again.

## 8 October 2026

**Crafting equipment no longer fails.**
Making a weapon, a shield or a piece of armour could fail, and a failed attempt
still consumed your materials. The higher the item level, the more often it
happened -- and past a certain level it could never succeed at all, however high
your crafting mastery was. That was never how it is supposed to work: crafting
always produces an item, and only the grade is left to chance. It does now. The
grade is still a lottery, exactly as before -- that part was correct the whole
time. Ammunition crafting is unchanged.

**You can put an expired auction item back on sale again.**
When an item came back from the auction house unsold, trying to list it again
answered with an error and nothing happened. It had never worked. The listing
goes through now, and we checked in game that the item is not duplicated along
the way: it stays a single item, exactly as before.

**Two combat fixes you may actually feel.**
Attack results were being sent to your client with two pieces of information in
the wrong order, so the client read them the wrong way round. And in siege
fights one value was missing from the message entirely, which shifted
everything after it. Both are corrected. Separately, defence was being
calculated from the wrong statistic -- it was reading your evasion where it
should have read your defence.

**Some Sette guards now drop ore.**
Sixteen of the guards around Sette now drop ore of mixed colours. Deserters
named Turncoat are not among them.

**The credits screen is ours now.**
Clicking Credits showed another operator's welcome message. It now says RF
NINJA, with the version. The original CCR credits are untouched -- they are the
people who made this game, and they stay.

**Restarts now log you out cleanly.**
When a restart is announced, you are now disconnected properly in the last
seconds before the server goes down, instead of being cut off. Your character
is saved on the way out, the same as a normal logout.

Changes made to the server and the client, newest first. The launcher applies
client updates automatically on startup.

**Guard towers now assist.**

**Missing sounds.**

## 4 October 2026

**The cure potion was wiping debuffs instead of shortening them.**
A player reported this, we told him it was working as intended, and he came
back a second time to say it was not. He was right. Using it now halves the
remaining duration of what is on you, rather than clearing it outright. The
description was never the thing that was wrong.

**Two new sort options at the auction house.**
You can now sort listings by *Ends soon* and by *Newest*, alongside the price
and level options that were already there. If you used the level sorts, check
them once and tell us if anything looks off.

**Archon-grade armour for levels 60, 65 and 70 is now on sale.**
The three hero merchants were stocking these sets up to level 55 and no
further, even though the higher pieces existed in full. All three tiers are
now available at the same counters.

**Chip wars last longer.**
The war crystals now take considerably more punishment before going down.
The intent is to give a race that loses the opening push the time to actually
mount a counter-attack, instead of the war being decided in the first
minutes.

**Six MAU ammunition types did nothing.**
They could be loaded and fired, and they were not linked to any weapon, so
they simply had no effect. They work now.

**Three different helmets all called "White Dragon Bone Head".**
They are a full helm, a pair of goggles and an amice, and they now say so.

**Archon helmets no longer show on male Bellato and Cora characters.**
This one is deliberate, and it is a step backwards that we chose on purpose.
These helmets had no artwork of their own for male characters — the original
developers never made any. What we had been doing was borrowing the look of
other helmets, which meant your Archon helmet looked like somebody else's
gear, and at some tiers like a beginner's headband. The official game does
not show them at all, so neither do we now. The helmets still work and still
give their stats; they are just not drawn.

**Two character texture packs.**
Updated default textures for female Cora and female Bellato.

## 1 October 2026

**Daily quest rewards you could not actually use.**
Several daily quests handed out an experience potion locked to a level range
below the quest's own. If you were at the top of the bracket, the potion simply
refused to be drunk — you had earned a reward you could not touch. Thirty
quests were affected. They now give the potion that matches their own level
range, the six highest-tier quests give one at all (they gave none), and the
quantities now match what the quests were meant to hand out. Several of them
also hand out a travel scroll and a crystal shard they were supposed to include
and did not.

**The Sympathy Charm did nothing at all.**
A player reported it, ran the comparisons himself, and he was right: the charm
had no effect on an animus whatsoever. This was not a value set too low — the
bonus it was designed to give had simply stopped being read by this version of
the game. We have reimplemented it. It now adds to your animus's attack while
the charm is in your inventory, it stacks up to a ceiling, and it is not
consumed. The charms are also on sale again at the Cora merchants, where they
had quietly stopped being stocked.

**Trade your hunting time for a Premium Pass.**
A new option at the premium NPC: if you are not premium, the time you spend
actually hunting can be exchanged for a pass. Five hours of hunting buys one
day of premium, so thirty-five hours for the seven-day pass. The counter
follows your account rather than a single character, it survives relogging, and
only time with real activity counts — standing still does not accumulate.

**Guard towers can no longer be placed almost on top of each other.**
We had loosened the minimum distance between two towers some weeks ago, after
it proved frustratingly large. Players pointed out it had gone too far the
other way. It is back to the original rule: a tower has to be placed clear of
another one's own radius.

**The server was stuttering, briefly but constantly.**
Short freezes, a fraction of a second, repeating. Everyone felt them at the
same moment because they came from the server itself, not from anyone's
connection. The cause was a setting on the machine that nobody had chosen — it
came from a default. Fixed, and measured before and after.


**A technical component of the client has been brought up to the current
official version.**
Nothing changes in how the game looks or plays — this is a background piece the
client loads at startup, and it is now the version shipped by the engine's
authors rather than the slightly older one we had been carrying. The launcher
applies it on its own; you do not need to reinstall anything.

Worth mentioning rather than leaving silent: this component sits close to the
graphics layer, and it is the first time we have updated it through the
launcher. It has been tested here and the game runs normally — but if you
notice anything odd at startup, or any display behaviour that was not there
before, please tell us on Discord or open an issue. We would rather hear about
it quickly than have someone struggle in silence.

## 27 September 2026
